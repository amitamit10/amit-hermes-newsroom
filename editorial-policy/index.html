Editorial desk / What we cover and how

Evidence first.
Hype last.

Coverage

We cover AI, agents, open-source tools, and developer workflows, including verified updates to Amit Elgabsi’s public projects. A scheduled cycle is an opportunity to publish, not a quota: a weak or duplicate story is skipped.

Evidence

Search snippets are leads, not proof. We prefer primary sources, inspect the underlying material, and corroborate material claims independently. Company claims, measurements, and newsroom analysis are labeled distinctly. Each factual claim links to its supporting source.

Authorship and AI

Show who did what.

  • Amit Elgabsi is the publisher and owns the publication decisions.
  • Hermes Agent may discover sources, draft, and run checks. AI-generated news reports are labeled; they are not described as personally reported or human-reviewed unless an article says so.
  • We do not invent interviews, tests, quotes, statistics, or Amit’s opinions.
  • Project status, upstream work, and visual provenance are identified in the story.

Corrections and sponsorships

Accountability stays visible.

  • Material corrections should identify what changed; the original publication time is preserved.
  • Paid placements are labeled as advertising or sponsorship and do not buy editorial coverage or ranking.
  • There is no live ad network connected in this Preview.

To report an error or discuss coverage, contact Amit through LinkedIn.
